# 0.3.1

## New Features

- The floating panels share one glass hierarchy and one bevel, and the "Glass transparency" setting now reaches every panel.
- The clipboard search field and its header buttons use real system glass, with a system-drawn edge.
- The clipboard history opens in the middle of the pointer's screen or near the cursor; choose under "Clipboard on the cursor screen".
- Each clipboard entry carries its own detail button, so one entry's detail opens without moving the selection.
- Section headings and the cards under them are spaced apart in the settings window.

## Bug Fixes

- The keystroke display can be dragged by any part of its panel, except the settings button.
- Clipboard action icons use a thinner outline, and the delete shortcut hint shows the backspace symbol again.
- Copying images no longer leaks memory.
- The soft-wrap switch could not be turned on or off; it works now.
- The clipboard search field no longer ignores clicks when the panel uses the frosted or glass material.
- The clipboard options popover no longer runs past the edge of the panel.
- The update notice's title is no longer cut off.

# 0.3.1

## 新增功能

- 浮窗共用同一套玻璃层级和同一道倒角，「玻璃透明度」设置现在对每个面板生效。

- 剪贴板历史在指针所在屏幕的中央或光标附近打开，可在「剪贴板在当前屏幕」中选择。

- 设置窗口的分区标题与下方卡片之间增加了间距。

## 问题修复

- 按键显示面板的任意位置都可以拖动，设置按钮除外。
- 剪贴板条目操作图标改用更细的线条样式，删除快捷键提示改回退格符号。
- 复制图片时不再泄漏内存。
- 软换行按钮无法正常开启或关闭的问题已修复。
- 使用磨砂或玻璃材质时，剪贴板搜索框不再忽略点击。
